Questions

Before you contact us

How fast will someone call me back?

A specialist reviews every request and calls you back as soon as possible. If you want to talk right now, call 1-888-779-2284.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Talking with us and getting your quote costs nothing. There is no pressure and no obligation.

Do I need measurements before I call?

No. Rough measurements and photos help if you have them. With full installation, we measure your space, doorways, drain and utilities for you.

Can I get a quote for a roll-in shower?

Yes. Our Bestbath roll-in showers include a 30-year warranty on the entire shower, plus a 1-year labor warranty with white-glove installation. Just mention it when you call or on the form.

Can I buy a tub and install it myself?

Yes. Choose supply only on the form. Hydro Dimensions supply-only purchases from us include a 1-year labor warranty. Most customers choose full installation so everything is handled.

Yes. Many of our calls come from adult children. We are happy to talk with you and your parent together and keep everyone updated.
