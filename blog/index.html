Quick answers

Walk-in tub questions we hear every day

Is a walk-in tub safer than a regular tub?

For many people, yes. A walk-in tub removes the tall wall you step over, gives you a seat at chair height and adds a textured floor and grab bars. It cannot promise no one will ever fall, but it takes the riskiest moves out of bath time.

Walk-in tub or roll-in shower?

Pick a walk-in tub if you can step over a low lip or slide onto a seat and you love to soak. Pick a roll-in shower if you use a wheelchair full time, need a caregiver beside you or prefer showers.

Which walk-in tub brand is best?

It depends on your needs. We sell Hydro Dimensions, Ella, Bestbath, RANE and Meditub. Hydro Dimensions is our pick for value because of its lifetime warranty on the whole tub. Ella has the widest size range. Meditub fits tight budgets and small spaces.

How long does a walk-in tub take to fill and drain?

It varies by tub and plumbing. Hydro Dimensions says its standard faucet can fill a tub in 4 to 6 minutes and its Safe-T Drain can empty it in as little as 85 seconds. Ella uses a gravity-driven dual drain.

Will my water heater keep up?

Deep tubs need lots of hot water. Ella lists a suggested water heater size for each model. We check your heater during your quote so your bath stays warm.

Do Florida condos allow walk-in tubs?

Often, yes, but many associations want approval first. Some limit work hours or elevator use. Check your condo or HOA rules early.
