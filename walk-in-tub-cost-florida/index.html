The short answer

Why there is no one price for a walk-in tub

You will see price ranges online. They are wide for a reason. A walk-in tub is two things at once. It is a product, and it is a small remodel.

The product cost depends on the brand, size, door and options. The remodel cost depends on your bathroom. Is the space the right size? Is the drain on the correct side? Is there power for jets? Is the water heater big enough?

Two neighbors can buy the same tub and pay very different totals. One has an easy swap. The other needs a new circuit and wall repairs.

That is why we do not post one price for everyone. A posted price would be wrong for most people. Instead, we give you a fast, accurate quote for your home.

Part 1: the tub

What affects the price of the tub itself

Brand

We sell Hydro Dimensions, Ella, Bestbath, RANE and Meditub. Each has its own build, features and warranty. Meditub is a budget-friendly option. Hydro Dimensions is our pick for value because of its lifetime warranty.

Size

Compact tubs cost less to make and hold less water. Wide, bariatric, extra-long and two-seat tubs cost more.

Door style

Inward swing, outward swing and wheelchair transfer doors are built differently. Transfer tubs with wide L-shaped doors often cost more than standard models.

Jets and therapy

A soaking tub has no jets. Air jets, water jets and dual systems each add cost. So do inline heaters and heated seats.

Faucets and drains

Fast-fill faucets and fast drains make bath time more comfortable. Upgraded finishes and color packages add cost too.

Warranty

A longer, broader warranty adds value. Hydro Dimensions covers the shell, frame and finish, motors, electronic controls and door seals for life. Fewer repair bills later can matter more than a lower price today.

Part 2: the installation

What affects the cost of installation

Installation is where Florida homes differ most. These are the items that move the total up or down.

Removing the old tub or shower

Most jobs start with demo and haul-away. Cast iron tubs are heavy and take more work to remove than fiberglass or acrylic ones.

Plumbing

Matching your current drain side keeps costs down. Many Florida homes sit on a concrete slab. Moving a drain there means cutting and patching concrete.

Electrical

Jets and heaters need proper power, usually a dedicated GFCI-protected circuit. A soaking tub skips this cost.

Walls and surround

Pulling out an old tub can expose worn tile or damaged wallboard. Wall panels or repairs around the new tub add to the job.

Water heater

Deep tubs need lots of hot water. If your heater is too small, a larger tank or second heater may be needed.

Upstairs and condo work

Upper floors may need a floor check. Condos may have HOA approval steps, elevator scheduling or work-hour rules.

Permits

Some plumbing or electrical work needs a permit. Ask who pulls it and whether it is in the quote.

How to compare walk-in tub quotes fairly

The lowest number is not always the lowest cost. Line up the details before you decide.

Same scope

Does each quote include removal, plumbing, electrical, walls and cleanup? A quote that leaves these out only looks cheaper.

Same tub features

Compare size, door style, seat width, jets, heater and drain. Small differences add up.

Warranty in writing

What covers the shell, the door seal, the motors and the labor? For how long? Does it shrink over time?

Water heater check

Did they check your water heater against the tub size? A lukewarm bath is a costly surprise.

Timeline

When will the tub arrive? How long will the install take? Hurricane season can affect schedules.

No pressure

Be careful with "sign today" discounts. A fair company gives you time to think and compare.

Help paying

Ways Floridians get help paying for a walk-in tub

Most people pay for a walk-in tub on their own. But several programs can help some families. Here is what is real, with links to official sources.

Medicare

Original Medicare generally does not pay for walk-in tubs, because bathroom safety items are not usually treated as covered medical equipment. Some Medicare Advantage plans offer bathroom safety benefits. Coverage varies, so call your plan. Medicare equipment rules.

SHINE Medicare counseling

Florida's SHINE program offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling. Reach it through the Elder Helpline at 1-800-963-5337. Florida Elder Affairs.

VA HISA grant

A lifetime benefit for medically necessary home changes. Up to $6,800 for a service-connected disability, or up to $2,000 for other eligible veterans. You need a VA prescription. Official VA HISA page.

County SHIP programs

Florida's State Housing Initiatives Partnership sends funds to counties and cities. Many run home repair programs, and accessibility work can qualify. Help is often a deferred or forgivable loan. Call your county or city housing office.

Area Agencies on Aging

Florida has 11 Area Agencies on Aging. The Elder Helpline at 1-800-963-5337 connects you to yours for local programs and caregiver help. Elder Helpline.

Medicaid long-term care screening

To be screened for Florida's Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care program, contact your local Aging and Disability Resource Center. Find your ADRC.

Medical expense tax deduction

IRS Publication 502 says home improvements made mainly for medical care can count as medical expenses, generally for the part of the cost above any increase in home value. Itemizing rules and income thresholds apply. IRS Publication 502.

Family help

Many adult children help a parent pay for a safer bathroom. We are glad to talk with the whole family, even if some of you live out of state.

Get the most for your money

Smart ways to control walk-in tub cost

  • Match your drain side. Keeping the drain where it is avoids cutting a slab.
  • Pick jets you will use. A soaking tub can be the best buy if you mainly want safety.
  • Choose the right size. A tub that fits your space avoids extra framing. A smaller tub needs less hot water.
  • Think long term. A lifetime warranty on motors and seals can prevent repair bills later.
  • Plan for the future. If mobility may change, the right choice now avoids a second remodel.
  • Check programs early. VA and county programs can take time. Start before you schedule.

Questions and answers

Walk-in tub cost FAQ

How much does a walk-in tub cost in Florida?

There is no single price. The cost depends on the tub brand and size, the door style, jets and heaters, and what your bathroom needs for installation. For a fast, accurate quote for your home, call 1-888-779-2284 or use our quote form.

Why don't you list walk-in tub prices online?

Two homes rarely need the same job. One tub drops into a 60-inch space. Another needs a new circuit, a moved drain or wall repairs. A posted price would be wrong for most people, so we quote your actual project.

What costs more, the tub or the installation?

It depends on the project. A simple tub swap keeps installation costs lower. Plumbing changes, electrical work, wall repairs or a water heater upgrade add to the total. Your quote shows both parts clearly.

Do jets make a walk-in tub cost more?

Yes. Air jets, water jets, dual systems, inline heaters and heated seats all add cost. They also need a proper electrical circuit. A soaking tub with no jets usually costs less.

Is it cheaper to install a walk-in tub myself?

Supply-only can lower the upfront cost if you have the skills. But the tub must be level, the door must seal and jets need a licensed electrician. Mistakes can cost more later. Hydro Dimensions supply-only purchases from us still include a 1-year labor warranty.

Does a lifetime warranty save money?

It can. Motors, controls and door seals are the parts most likely to need service over the years. Hydro Dimensions covers the shell, frame and finish, motors, electronic controls, door seals and supporting parts with a lifetime warranty.

Does Medicare pay for a walk-in tub in Florida?

Original Medicare generally does not pay for walk-in tubs. Some Medicare Advantage plans offer bathroom safety benefits, but coverage varies by plan and county. Call your plan and ask before you buy. Florida's free SHINE program can help you understand your Medicare options.

Can veterans get help paying for a walk-in tub?

Veterans may qualify for the VA HISA grant. It is a lifetime benefit for medically necessary home changes, up to $6,800 for a service-connected disability or up to $2,000 for other eligible veterans. You need a prescription from your VA provider and apply through VA.

What is the Florida SHIP program?

SHIP is Florida's State Housing Initiatives Partnership. The state sends funds to counties and cities, and each one runs its own home repair program. Accessibility work can qualify. Help is often a deferred or forgivable loan, and income limits apply.

Can I deduct a walk-in tub on my taxes?

Possibly. IRS Publication 502 says home improvements made mainly for medical care can count as medical expenses, generally for the part of the cost above any increase in your home's value. Medical expenses must be itemized and generally must exceed 7.5% of your adjusted gross income. Ask a tax professional.

What should a walk-in tub quote include?

A good quote lists the tub model and options, removal of the old tub or shower, plumbing, electrical, wall or surround work, cleanup, the timeline and the warranty. Compare the same scope when you compare prices.
