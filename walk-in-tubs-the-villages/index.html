Sumter, Lake and Marion counties

A community made for aging in place

The Villages is a large age-restricted community in Central Florida that spreads across Sumter, Lake and Marion counties. Its homes were designed with older residents in mind: single-story layouts, attached garages and walkable or golf-cart-friendly neighborhoods. Even so, many bathrooms were built with standard tubs, and a standard tub wall gets harder to step over with every passing year.

We help homeowners in and around The Villages choose and install walk-in tubs, wheelchair transfer tubs and roll-in showers. We do not have a showroom in The Villages. We plan by phone and photos, measure in your home and install from start to finish.

Housing

Home styles in The Villages

Homes here range from smaller patio villas and courtyard villas to larger designer homes. Almost all are single-story on concrete slabs. That combination is good news for a walk-in tub: no upper-floor weight concerns and no stairs, just a straightforward bathroom swap if the drain side matches.

Villas and smaller homes

Villas often have one or two compact bathrooms. A standard 60-inch tub alcove can usually take a walk-in tub. If the only tub is in the guest bath, consider which bathroom you use every day before deciding where to put it.

Larger designer homes

Larger homes may have a primary bath with a walk-in shower and a hall bath with a tub. Some owners convert the hall tub to a walk-in tub and keep the shower. Others turn the shower into a low-threshold roll-in.

Older sections

The oldest parts of the community date back decades. Original water heaters and tile may be near the end of their life, so we check both.

Garage access

Attached garages make delivery easier. We measure the door from the garage into the house and the hallway to the bathroom.

Planning

Which bathroom should get the change?

Think about where you bathe on an ordinary Tuesday, not where guests stay. The best spot for a walk-in tub or accessible shower is the bathroom closest to where you sleep, with enough room to turn with a walker.

  • Will the bather want to soak, or mainly shower?
  • Is a wheelchair or walker part of daily life now, or likely soon?
  • Does a spouse have different needs?
  • Do grandchildren visit and need a regular tub?

Our tub vs shower guide helps you weigh it.

Two bathers

When spouses have different needs

Many households here are couples, and it is common for one partner to love a long soak while the other can no longer step over anything. One bathroom rarely serves both perfectly. A few approaches work well in two-bath homes:

  • Split the baths. A walk-in tub in one bathroom and a low-threshold or roll-in shower in the other.
  • A transfer tub. A wheelchair transfer tub lets a seated bather slide in, while still giving a full soak to the partner who wants one.
  • Shower first, tub later. If one partner's needs are urgent, start there and plan the second bathroom next.

Every option still needs grab bars where both people's hands go. We are glad to talk with both of you together.

Community rules

Community rules, contractors and access

The Villages has deed restrictions and community standards, and most of them focus on what you can see from outside the home. An interior bathroom change usually does not affect the exterior. Still, check your deed restrictions and any rules about contractor vehicles, work hours and debris containers before install day.

Contractor vehicles

Work trucks and trailers may have parking limits on narrow streets. We plan the delivery and debris haul-away to keep the street clear.

Permits

Plumbing and electrical permits, if needed, come from the county or city where your home sits: Sumter, Lake or Marion. Your installer should know which applies.

Questions and answers

The Villages walk-in tub FAQ

Will a walk-in tub fit in a villa in The Villages?

Often, yes. Many villas have a standard tub alcove that fits a walk-in tub. Compact models exist for smaller spaces. We measure the alcove, the drain side and the path from the garage before recommending one.

Do I need approval from The Villages to install a walk-in tub?

Interior bathroom changes usually do not affect exterior standards, but check your deed restrictions and any rules on contractor parking and debris. Permits, if needed, come from your county or city.

Which Area Agency on Aging serves The Villages?

Sumter, Lake and Marion counties are served by the Mid-Florida Area Agency on Aging, also known as Elder Options. The Elder Helpline at 1-800-963-5337 connects you.

What if my spouse and I need different setups?

Many couples split the bathrooms: a walk-in tub in one and a low-threshold or roll-in shower in the other. A wheelchair transfer tub can also work for a seated bather and a partner who wants a full soak.
