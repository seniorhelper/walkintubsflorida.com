Snowbirds

A walk-in tub plan for seasonal residents

  1. Start by phone

    Send photos of the bathroom, tub, doorways and electrical panel, plus the six measurements. We can narrow down models before you arrive.

  2. Handle approvals early

    Associations often need weeks to approve alteration requests. Submit the application while you are still away so work can start when you are back.

  3. Install while you are here

    Be home for the walkthrough so you learn how the door, faucet and drain work. Before you leave for the summer, run the tub and leave the door open to dry.

Closing up for summer? Leave the bathroom fan or air conditioning set to control humidity. Many residents use a dehumidifying setting while away.

Community living

55+ condos and golf communities

Palm Beach County has many age-restricted and golf communities, from large condo developments such as Century Village in West Palm Beach to gated single-family neighborhoods in Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach and Wellington.

In condos, expect an alteration application, proof of license and insurance, and rules about work hours and elevator use. In gated communities, contractors usually need gate passes, and some HOAs restrict where work trucks and dumpsters can park. These are easy to handle when they are known up front.

Questions for your association

  • Do you require an application for bathroom plumbing work?
  • What insurance certificate do you need from the contractor?
  • Are there seasonal work restrictions?
  • How do we reserve the service elevator or gate access?
Paperwork

The condo document checklist

Palm Beach County associations vary, but the paperwork requests are similar from building to building. Gathering it in one folder before you apply saves back-and-forth with the management office and keeps the install date on track.

  • The association's alteration or remodeling application, signed by the owner
  • A written scope of work: remove tub, install walk-in tub, plumbing and electrical connections, wall finish
  • The contractor's Florida license number
  • A certificate of insurance naming the association, if required
  • Any permit numbers, once issued
  • Proposed work dates and hours
  • Elevator reservation and floor-protection plan
  • A plan for hauling away the old tub and debris

Ask about timing: some boards meet monthly. Find out the next meeting date and the cut-off for applications so your request is not held a month.

Housing notes

Homes across Palm Beach County

Historic West Palm and Lake Worth Beach

Older neighborhoods have 1920s to 1950s homes with original tile, plaster walls and smaller doorways. We measure the delivery path carefully and look at the walls behind the old tub.

Suburban ranch homes

Single-story block homes on slabs are common in Greenacres, Royal Palm Beach and Boynton Beach. Matching the drain side keeps the job simple.

Coastal towers

Oceanfront and Intracoastal condos in places like Singer Island, Highland Beach and Juno Beach bring building rules and flood-zone considerations. See our climate guide.

Jupiter and the north county

Newer homes often have larger primary bathrooms with room for a wider walk-in tub or a separate roll-in shower.

Questions and answers

West Palm Beach walk-in tub FAQ

Can you plan my walk-in tub while I am out of state?

Yes. We can work through models, measurements and a quote by phone and photos, then schedule installation for when you are back in Florida so you can attend the walkthrough.

Do condo associations in Palm Beach County allow walk-in tubs?

Many do, with approval. Expect an application, proof of license and insurance, and rules on work hours and elevator use. Some buildings limit noisy work during the winter season.

Which Area Agency on Aging serves Palm Beach County?

The Area Agency on Aging of Palm Beach/Treasure Coast serves Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie, Indian River and Okeechobee counties.

How far ahead should I apply to my condo board?

Ask the management office how often the board meets and the deadline for applications. Some boards meet monthly, so applying a few weeks before you want work to start avoids a month of waiting.
