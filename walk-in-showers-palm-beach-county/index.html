Cost

How much does a walk-in shower cost in Palm Beach County?

Aging Safely Baths publishes $13,500 to $17,000 installed for a roll-in or low-threshold accessible shower, after current promotions. The low end is a pan and wall system that fits the old tub space and uses the existing drain. The high end is a larger one-piece shower, or a job where the floor has to change to get a true roll-in entry. Walk-in tubs, for comparison, run $15,250 to $20,000 installed.

Those numbers are for the whole job: taking out the tub, hauling it away, setting and leveling the pan, the walls, the valve and handheld shower, a seat and grab bars where the bather needs them, and the walkthrough. Glass is optional. A weighted curtain leaves a wider opening for a shower chair and nothing to squeegee.

The step

How much lower is a shower entry than a tub wall?

The side of a regular bathtub is the tallest step in many homes. Kohler's spec sheet for its Bellwether alcove tub lists a 14-inch apron, and Kohler calls that height "easy entry." Its walk-in bath brings the step down to 3 inches. A roll-in shower built to the ADA reference allows a threshold of no more than half an inch, beveled, which a wheelchair or rolling shower chair can cross.

That difference is why many Palm Beach County families skip the tub altogether. Someone who shuffles after a stroke, uses a rollator, or needs a helper to steady them often does better with no lip to lift a foot over. Someone who loves a warm soak and steps well may still prefer a walk-in tub.

Permits

Which office issues the permit for my address?

Palm Beach County is a patchwork of cities, towns and villages, and most run their own building departments. The county's Planning, Zoning and Building (PZB) Permit Center handles only the unincorporated area. A shower conversion is plumbing work, so expect a permit wherever you live; the licensed installer normally pulls it and lists it on the quote.

Condo or house

What changes when the bathroom is in a condo?

In a single-family house the bathroom floor is a slab on the ground, and the question is simply whether to cut it. In a mid-rise or high-rise condo, the floor under your tub is also the ceiling of the unit below. The tub drain passes through that slab, and so will the shower drain.

The simplest condo conversion keeps the drain in place: a low-threshold pan sized to the old 60-inch tub space, with the drain at the same end. Moving the drain to the center of a larger shower can mean coring new holes in a post-tensioned or reinforced slab, and associations often ask for a structural engineer's letter before they allow it. Ask the manager two questions before you fall in love with a layout: "May the drain move?" and "What do you need from us if it does?"

The rest of the condo paperwork (alteration application, insurance certificate, work hours, elevator) is the same as for a tub. Our Palm Beach County walk-in tub guide has the document checklist.

Houses: block walls and slabs

Many single-story houses in Royal Palm Beach, Greenacres and Wellington sit on slabs with concrete block exterior walls. A shower against an outside wall is usually fine; the valve just goes on an interior wall. If the drain has to move for a roomier roll-in, the installer saws and patches the slab.

Seasonal owners

Do the quote and the association application from up north, then book the install for your first weeks back. Before you leave in spring, run the shower for a minute so the drain trap is full, and ask whoever checks the house to run it monthly so the trap never dries out.

Paying for it

Can Medicaid or the VA help pay for a walk-in shower in Florida?

Sometimes, and never automatically. Apply before work starts, because programs rarely pay for a finished job.

  • VA HISA: roll-in showers are the VA's own example of an essential bathroom change. Lifetime benefit $6,800 for service-connected conditions (or some other conditions for veterans rated 50 percent or more), $2,000 for other conditions. Spa and hot tub features are excluded.
  • Florida Medicaid long-term care: enrollees in the SMMC long-term care program may be able to get home accessibility adaptations through their plan. Our SMMC LTC bathroom guide walks through the steps.
  • Local repair help: some cities and the county run SHIP-funded repair programs. See SHIP home repair by county.
  • Original Medicare generally does not pay for showers or walk-in tubs.

Questions and answers

Palm Beach County walk-in shower FAQ

How much does a walk-in shower cost in Palm Beach County?

The published installed price for an accessible roll-in or low-threshold shower from Aging Safely Baths is $13,500 to $17,000 after current promotions, and the bigger one-piece units cost the most. In Palm Beach County, condo drain moves, tile and wall repair are what usually change a quote.

Can a condo bathtub be turned into a walk-in shower?

Often, yes. The easiest condo conversion uses a pan whose drain lines up with the old tub drain, because the drain sits in the concrete slab above the unit below. Moving it usually needs association approval and sometimes an engineer's sign-off, so ask the board first.

Who issues the permit in Palm Beach Gardens or Jupiter?

Each city does. Palm Beach Gardens permits go through its Building Division at 10500 N. Military Trail, and Jupiter's Building Department is at 210 Military Trail. Unincorporated addresses use the county PZB Permit Center on N. Jog Road. The licensed installer normally pulls the permit.

How long does a walk-in shower install take?

Most of our installs take one day once the shower system is on site. A condo job with association scheduling rules, a moved drain or new tile can take longer, and the written quote states the expected timeline.

Does the VA help pay for a roll-in shower in Florida?

It may. Roll-in showers are the VA's own example of an essential bathroom change under the HISA grant, which pays up to $6,800 over a lifetime for service-connected conditions and up to $2,000 for other conditions, once a VA provider documents the medical need and the project is approved.

Yes. We install across Palm Beach County, including Royal Palm Beach, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Tequesta, West Palm Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach and Boca Raton. Call 1-888-779-2284 with your ZIP code.
